Where we take you

Beyond Kenya, across East Africa

Our journeys also extend beyond Kenya into the wider East African region. In neighboring Tanzania, Uganda, and Rwanda, travelers can experience some of the continent’s most remarkable wildlife encounters within the vibrant East African countries.

From the vast plains and legendary wildlife migrations of Tanzania to the ancient rainforests of Uganda and Rwanda, East Africa offers extraordinary opportunities for gorilla and chimpanzee trekking. Walking through mist-covered forests to encounter mountain gorillas or tracking chimpanzees through lush jungles are among the most profound wildlife experiences on earth.

These experiences complement the classic safari, allowing travelers to witness not only the sweeping landscapes of the savannah but also the intimate world of the forests where our closest relatives in the animal kingdom live.
